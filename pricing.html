FAQS

What does it mean to be a "film-first" photographer? Why film?
"Film-first" means exactly that: film is my primary medium during every session, wedding, and weekend - not an upcharge or afterthought. Each exposure on film requires un-hurrying, which in turn leads to catching beautiful, unplanned moments.
Do you take on weddings on short notice?
Absolutely! My husband and I planned our entire wedding in less than 7 months, so we don’t believe one-size-fits-all. Whether it’s a big bash or an elopement, drop me a line!
What’s the turnaround time for a wedding gallery?
Generally, 4-6 weeks, although lab waits can occasionally delay that.
Do you charge extra for travel outside of MN?
I don’t charge any additional fees for travel anywhere within the U.S.! The price listed is the price you pay, plain and simple.
Is there a deposit with booking?
Yes! A signed contract and a 50% non-refundable deposit (applied towards the total payment) is needed to lock in your date. The final 50% will be due 30 days before the day of your wedding. You can also choose to pay in full at the time of contract signing.
What does the booking process look like?
Step 1: the inquiry. You inquire here! Within 24 hours, I’ll reach out with additional info and some timeslots to schedule a consult call.

Step 2: the consult call. We (me, you and your significant other) jump on a call together! I’ll walk through my approach, and you’ll fill me in on y’all’s story (personally, my favorite part!). This is a great way for us to get to know one another and for you to decide if I’m a good fit for YOU both!

Step 3: the deposit & contract. Once you've confirmed you want to move forward with booking, I’ll send over the contract and details for your deposit (or full payment if you opt to go that route). Once your deposit is paid, you’ll receive a confirmation email letting you know your date is booked. Time to let the good times roll!
What’s your approach to the wedding day?
Bespoke portraits alongside evocative captures of unplanned moments - all on 35mm & Instax - is my wheelhouse. My goal, whether the moment is prompted or completely unscripted, is to give you a gallery full of shots that make you say “I didn’t even know I wanted to remember that!”
