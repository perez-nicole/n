ABOUT ME.

I LIKE FILM
A LOT.

I got my start in photography as a kid with a plastic point-and-shoot Pikachu film camera in the 2000s. If you're thinking, "a camera that prints Pokemon borders on all your exposures doesn't really count" - well, you'd be 100% right. In high school, though, I finally moved up to a chunky solid-metal Pentax & the rest is history.

I MARRIED MY BEST FRIEND.

Other things to know about me? For starters, I probably wouldn’t even be doing this today if it weren’t for my husband Cristian. He’s my better half 100% (& my personal comedian). And a fun tidbit? He’s also a freelance photographer & often helps me out on gigs these days!

JESUS IS IT.

Jesus is it & He’s blessed us way too much not to give it all back. When we’re not out shooting, you can find us hanging out with our church fam @ Miracle City Church & helping out our local community.
